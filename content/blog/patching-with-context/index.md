A vulnerability score is useful context, but it is not the complete decision. Exposure, reachable code, available mitigations, and business impact determine what should happen next.

## Make the decision explicit

Record why an issue is fixed now, deferred, or accepted. A clear decision is easier to revisit than a silent exception buried in a ticket queue.
